About

I am a robotics and mechanical engineer with an M.S. from Duke University. I enjoy the hands-on work of bringing a system together: designing a mechanism, connecting its electronics, making sense of its sensors, and getting the software to move real hardware.

My projects span robotic manipulation, embedded sensing, assistive devices, and human-robot interaction. I document what I built, what I tested, and what still needs work so others can understand and build on it.
